<p class="meta">2024</p>
<h3>ABACBS Outstanding PhD Thesis Award 2024</h3>
<p>My PhD thesis received the 2024 Outstanding PhD Thesis Award from the Australian Bioinformatics and Computational Biology Society (ABACBS).</p>
<p>My PhD thesis titled &quot;Metagenomics Binning Using Assembly Graphs&quot; introduced the use of assembly graphs in metagenomic binning and explored new algorithms and computational models to bin contigs.</p>

<p class="meta">2023</p>
<h3>President's Award for Scientific Research 2023</h3>
<p>A first-authored paper from my bachelor thesis work was awarded a President's Award for Scientific Research 2023 in recognition of highly rated scientific research.</p>
<p>The paper titled &quot;Change Detection and Notification of Web Pages: A Survey&quot; was published in the prestigious journal ACM Computing Surveys (2023 Impact Factor: 23.8, ranked 1/143 in Computer Science Theory &amp; Methods).</p>
