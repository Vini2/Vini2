<section class="content-block">
<h2>Published Projects</h2>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="item-card">
<p class="meta">Interactive visual analytics</p>
<h3>GraphBin-Viz</h3>
<p>A browser-based framework for exploring and comparing graph-based metagenomic binning results.</p>
</article>
<article class="item-card">
<p class="meta">Assembly graph toolkit</p>
<h3>GraphBin-Tk</h3>
<p>A comprehensive toolkit combining GraphBin, GraphBin2, MetaCoAG, visualization, and evaluation utilities.</p>
</article>
<article class="item-card">
<p class="meta">Metaproteomics</p>
<h3>ConDiGA</h3>
<p>A taxonomic annotation pipeline for accurate protein sequence database construction from metagenomic data.</p>
</article>
<article class="item-card">
<p class="meta">Viral metagenomics</p>
<h3>Phables</h3>
<p>A flow-based approach for resolving high-quality bacteriophage genomes from fragmented assemblies.</p>
</article>
<article class="item-card">
<p class="meta">Metagenomic binning</p>
<h3>MetaCoAG</h3>
<p>A contig-binning tool using composition, coverage, and assembly graph connectivity information.</p>
</article>
<article class="item-card">
<p class="meta">Assembly graph bin refinement</p>
<h3>GraphBin</h3>
<p>A metagenomic contig-binning refinement tool based on assembly graph connectivity and label propagation.</p>
</article>
