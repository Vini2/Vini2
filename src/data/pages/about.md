<h2>My Background</h2>
<p>I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the <a href="https://cgg-anu.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">ANU Computational Genomics Group</a> at the <a href="http://www.anu.edu.au/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Australian National University</a>, under the supervision of <a href="https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~u1024708/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Dr Yu Lin</a>. My <a href="https://doi.org/10.25911/STAT-HC66" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">PhD thesis</a> focused on graph-based computational models and algorithms for metagenomic binning.</p>
<p>I also hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the <a href="https://cse.mrt.ac.lk/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Department of Computer Science and Engineering</a>, <a href="https://uom.lk/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">University of Moratuwa</a>, Sri Lanka. My final year research project (equivalent to an Honours project) focused on computational methods for change detection and notification in web pages.</p>
